# Positive Rate V2 List

Wish list for future updates. Nothing here ships in V1.

## Rental protection

Removed from V1 on Oct 31, 2026. It was a $9 per flight hour fee on bookings that include a plane, but no insurance policy or claims process was behind it.

To bring it back, choose how it's backed:

- **Insurance partner:** a group policy or per-flight renter's coverage from an aviation insurer (for example AVEMCO), paid for by the fee.
- **Damage waiver:** caps what a renter owes an owner for damage, paid from a reserve Positive Rate holds. This may count as selling insurance.
- **Bring your own:** no fee; renters show proof of renter's insurance when they sign up.

Before charging for it, check with an aviation insurance broker or attorney: charging for damage "protection" may need an insurance license in Florida.

To switch it back on: set `protectionPerHr` in `src/pricing.js`, run `node scripts/sync-shared.js`, and redeploy `book` and `booking-action`. The app, the server and the payout split already handle the fee.

## Solo rental damage deposit

Removed from V1 on Oct 31, 2026. The app promised a $500 hold on the renter's card for every solo rental, but the server never placed it.

To build it:

- At confirm (or 24 hours before the rental), place a $500 hold on the renter's saved card with a manual-capture PaymentIntent.
- At close-out, release the hold, or capture part of it if the owner reports damage.
- Card holds expire after about 7 days, so place them close to the rental date rather than at booking.
- Decide who handles damage disputes between renter and owner, and how.

To switch the display back on: set `deposit` in `src/pricing.js`, run `node scripts/sync-shared.js`, and redeploy `book` and `booking-action`. The app already shows the hold when `deposit` is above 0, but the server code to place and release it still has to be written.

## Payout status webhooks (Accounts v2)

Payout accounts are now created with Stripe's Accounts v2 API. The app checks payout status when the instructor returns from Stripe and again at every close-out, so nothing waits on a webhook. Later, subscribe a Stripe event destination to the v2 thin event `v2.core.account[configuration.recipient].capability_status_updated` so status updates (and release of held pay) also happen when the instructor never reopens the app.
